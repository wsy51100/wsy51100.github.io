01

Background & Objectives

Background

With the rapid development of social economy and the continuous development of digital technology, scientific, refined and cost-effective spatial information technology has become the only choice for the integration of social economy and human information. By combining spatial information technology with the protection of ancient memorial archway, the protection and utilization of ancient memorial archway buildings can provide local cultural relics management departments with new ideas and measures for the protection of ancient memorial archway.

Objectives

As the carrier of the cultural information of the times, the ancient memorial archway contains ancient folk culture, architectural culture and other information, and has high historical research and protection value. Jimei Archway in Fengxin County is the only four pillar and four sided stone memorial archway in Jiangxi Province. Its profound history and exquisite architectural skills show a high cultural value. Taking Jimei Archway in Fengxin County as an example, this paper discusses the use of spatial information technology to digitally display Jimei Archway, and further studies the application of digital technology in the protection of ancient memorial archways.

Workflow

Field acquisition was followed by data screening, reconstruction, and export. After unsuitable photographs were removed, image correspondences, camera parameters, and positioning information provided the basis for estimating the scene geometry and producing a textured model.

Research workflow

  • On-site scanning and photography

Data preparation

Blurred, overexposed, and irrelevant photographs were removed. Camera metadata were checked, and images were grouped by shared camera parameters, preparing a consistent image set for aerotriangulation.
